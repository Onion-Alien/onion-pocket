**0.2.2: your key stays on your phone.** With Onion Board 1.9.4 or newer the phone no longer sends its key over the Wi-Fi with every tap. It signs each tap with the key instead, and Onion Board takes each signature only once, so someone reading your Wi-Fi's traffic can't copy the key or replay a tap. A phone whose clock is a few minutes off still works. With an older Onion Board it works as before.

**0.2.1:** opening Settings → Remote no longer flashes a small blank window up on the desktop for a moment (the *Let it through Windows Firewall…* button showed before it was part of the card).

**0.2.0: the radio and the sound on your phone.** Two new tabs at the bottom of the phone page. **Radio:** play and stop the radio, your Favorites, Recent and Popular stations or a search, a random station, star one, Live / Only me, Hear it myself and the radio's volume. **Sound:** the live speed and pitch, bass, treble, muffle, reverb, echo, distortion and the presets, Reset all, and Who's listening (Discord, Steam voice…). What the phone changes shows on the PC's own sliders. The new tabs need Onion Board 1.7.2 or newer; with an older one you get the pads as before.

**0.1.1:** ticking *Let phones on this Wi-Fi use it* no longer freezes Onion Board while Windows asks about the firewall. The card says to answer Windows' prompt, and phones can connect as soon as you do. Before, the whole app stopped responding until the prompt was answered, and a prompt hidden behind another window looked like a hang.

Your pads on your phone. Scan the code on Onion Board's Settings → Remote with your phone's camera and tap a pad on the phone: it plays on the PC, into Discord or your game like any pad. iPhone or Android, in the browser: nothing to install on the phone.

Get it from Onion Board itself: Settings → Remote → *Get Onion Pocket*. It needs an Onion Board newer than 1.6.8.
